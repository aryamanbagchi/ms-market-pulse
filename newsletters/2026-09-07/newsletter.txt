EDITOR'S TAKE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Novartis delivered the week's defining breakthrough with positive Phase 3 data
for its BTK inhibitor Rhapsido (remibrutinib), which significantly reduced MS
disease progression and relapses without triggering liver safety signals. This
success intensifies a high-stakes competitive push around oral BTK inhibitors
targeting disease progression, where Roche is advancing fenebrutinib in
relapsing and primary progressive MS, and Zenas BioPharma is initiating global
Phase 3 trials for orelabrutinib in progressive populations. Concurrently,
Roche is defending its dominant MS position by expanding subcutaneous
ocrelizumab into pediatric populations and securing a Phase 3 trial waiver for
a higher-dose regimen. Strategy teams should now monitor Novartis' forthcoming
detailed trial disclosures to assess whether Rhapsido's efficacy and safety
profile can unseat Roche's established franchise across both relapsing and
progressive indications.
