EDITOR'S TAKE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The competitive landscape for progressive multiple sclerosis is sharpening as
next-generation oral BTK inhibitors mount a concentrated push into hard-to-
treat disease forms. Zenas BioPharma launched two global Phase 3 trials for
orelabrutinib across primary and non-active secondary progressive MS, while
Novartis initiated a parallel Phase 3 SPMS study for remibrutinib, signaling a
decisive industry alignment on targeting disability accrual. Concurrently,
Roche is defending its market position by advancing a high-dose ocrelizumab
Phase 3 relapsing MS trial into active status to test whether dose escalation
yields superior clinical efficacy. This aggressive pipeline movement occurs
against a backdrop of legacy franchise decay, highlighted by Biogen reporting
that non-MS revenues have officially overtaken its foundational MS business.
Industry observers should now monitor long-term trial execution across these
progressive MS cohorts to evaluate if BTK inhibition can finally establish a
therapeutic foothold where historic options remain limited.

1. A Study of Orelabrutinib in Patients With Primary Progressive Multiple
Sclerosis
   Impact 4/5 | ClinicalTrials.gov | 31 Jul 2026 | Phase 3 | Recruiting |
   Zenas BioPharma (USA), LLC | NCT07067463

   Zenas BioPharma has initiated a global Phase 3 randomized, double-blind
   study (NCT07067463) evaluating its CNS-penetrable BTK inhibitor,
   orelabrutinib, versus placebo in approximately 705 patients with primary
   progressive multiple sclerosis (PPMS). Subjects will be randomized 2:1 and
   treated for 30 to 60 months with a minimum treatment duration of 12 months.

   WHY IT MATTERS: The trial advances another CNS-penetrant BTK inhibitor into
   pivotal development for PPMS, intensifying competition against established
   anti-CD20 therapies and rival BTK programs targeting progressive MS
   populations.
   https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07067463

2. A Study to Evaluate the Efficacy, Safety and Pharmacokinetics (PK) of a
Higher Dose of Ocrelizumab in Adults With Relapsing Multiple Sclerosis (RMS)
   Impact 4/5 | ClinicalTrials.gov | 30 Jul 2026 | Phase 3 | Active Not
   Recruiting | Hoffmann-La Roche | NCT04544436

   Hoffmann-La Roche's Phase 3 trial evaluating a higher intravenous dose of
   ocrelizumab administered every 24 weeks versus the approved 600 mg dose in
   adults with relapsing multiple sclerosis (RMS) has reached active, not
   recruiting status. The randomized, double-blind, parallel-group,
   multicenter study is designed to assess the efficacy, safety, and
   pharmacokinetics of the higher-dose regimen. The trial outcome will
   determine whether an escalated ocrelizumab dosing schedule offers superior
   clinical outcomes over the standard 600 mg regimen in RMS.

   WHY IT MATTERS: A successful higher-dose ocrelizumab readout could enable
   Roche to optimize efficacy outcomes and protect its anti-CD20 franchise
   share against competing RMS therapies from Novartis, Sanofi, and TG
   Therapeutics.
   https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04544436

3. A Study of Orelabrutinib in Patients With Secondary Progressive Multiple
Sclerosis
   Impact 4/5 | ClinicalTrials.gov | 30 Jul 2026 | Phase 3 | Recruiting |
   Zenas BioPharma (USA), LLC | NCT07299019

   Zenas BioPharma has initiated recruitment for a global Phase 3 study
   (NCT07299019) evaluating orelabrutinib, a CNS-penetrable BTK inhibitor, in
   patients with non-active Secondary Progressive Multiple Sclerosis (spMS).
   The randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial plans to enroll
   approximately 990 subjects in a 2:1 ratio, with treatment lasting 24 to 60
   months.

   WHY IT MATTERS: This trial positions Zenas BioPharma's orelabrutinib as a
   direct Phase 3 competitor in the non-active spMS landscape targeting
   progressive MS pathways via BTK inhibition.
   https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07299019
