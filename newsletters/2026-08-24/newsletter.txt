EDITOR'S TAKE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Zenas BioPharma’s aggressive Phase 3 expansion of orelabrutinib into
progressive MS populations marks the week's most consequential strategic move,
signaling a push to capture non-active secondary progressive and primary
progressive indications where therapeutic options remain severely limited.
This push reinforces a broader industry pattern centered on late-stage BTK
inhibitor progression, evidenced by Novartis reaching active status in its
Phase 3 REMODEL-1 trial for remibrutinib and Roche advancing Phase 2 trials
for fenebrutinib. Concurrently, TG Therapeutics is fortifying its anti-CD20
franchise by advancing multiple clinical trials for ublituximab, targeting
subcutaneous delivery via autoinjector, modified dosing regimens, and
pediatric populations to rival established blockbusters. Meanwhile, Novartis
is defending its market share through a new observational study directly
comparing the real-world effectiveness and economic burden of Kesimpta against
Roche's Ocrevus. Moving forward, stakeholders should monitor whether upcoming
late-stage data readouts for these oral BTK inhibitors can demonstrate
sufficient efficacy in progressive forms of MS to successfully challenge the
dominant anti-CD20 paradigm.
