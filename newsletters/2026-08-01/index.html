Editor's Take

This week's primary market development is the aggressive clinical expansion of central nervous system (CNS)-penetrable BTK inhibitors into progressive MS populations, marked by Zenas BioPharma initiating Phase 3 trials for orelabrutinib in both primary progressive MS and non-active secondary progressive MS, alongside Novartis launching a Phase 3 study for remibrutinib in secondary progressive MS. This collective push into non-relapsing indications directly threatens Roche’s anti-CD20 dominance, forcing the incumbent to defend its franchise via active Phase 3 higher-dose intravenous ocrelizumab trials and Genentech's Phase 4 subcutaneous ocrelizumab switch study. Meanwhile, the anti-CD20 class faces dual pricing and competitive pressure, evidenced by biosimilar threats from Amgen’s active Phase 3 trial for ABP 692 and patient access risks from Australian PBS drug delisting disputes. Concurrently, Biogen’s financial reporting confirms a structural revenue rotation away from legacy MS assets as non-MS therapies overtake its franchise performance. Going forward, industry stakeholders must monitor how upcoming data from high-dose and subcutaneous anti-CD20 formulations hold up against the rapid late-stage progression of BTK candidates in non-relapsing disease settings.
