Editor's Take

A major clinical push into progressive multiple sclerosis is underway as BTK inhibitors target non-relapsing populations with high unmet need. Zenas BioPharma initiated dual Phase 3 trials evaluating its CNS-penetrable candidate orelabrutinib in primary and non-active secondary progressive MS, while Novartis launched its own Phase 3 study for remibrutinib in SPMS. Concurrently, the anti-CD20 landscape is shifting as Roche advances a high-dose ocrelizumab regimen to extend product defense against emerging competition like Amgen's biosimilar candidate, ABP 692. On the commercial front, Australian policy decisions exempted MS therapies from mandatory price cuts, contrasting with Biogen's latest earnings where non-MS revenues eclipsed its declining legacy MS business. Strategy teams must now watch enrollment trajectories across these late-stage BTK trials to see if the mechanism can finally deliver meaningful efficacy in progressive disease.

A Study of Orelabrutinib in Patients With Primary Progressive Multiple Sclerosis IMPACT 4/5
ClinicalTrials.gov · 31 Jul 2026 · Phase 3 · Recruiting · Zenas BioPharma (USA), LLC · NCT07067463

Zenas BioPharma has initiated a global Phase 3, randomized, double-blind study evaluating its CNS-penetrable BTK inhibitor, orelabrutinib, against placebo in patients with primary progressive multiple sclerosis (PPMS). The trial (NCT07067463) aims to enroll approximately 705 subjects randomized 2:1 to receive treatment for 30 to 60 months, with a minimum duration of 12 months.

Why it matters
Zenas BioPharma's advance of orelabrutinib into Phase 3 PPMS trials intensifies competition in the progressive MS space against rival oral BTK inhibitors from Sanofi, Roche, and Merck KGaA.

A Study of Orelabrutinib in Patients With Secondary Progressive Multiple Sclerosis IMPACT 4/5
ClinicalTrials.gov · 30 Jul 2026 · Phase 3 · Recruiting · Zenas BioPharma (USA), LLC · NCT07299019

Zenas BioPharma has initiated a global Phase 3 study (NCT07299019) evaluating orelabrutinib, a CNS-penetrable BTK inhibitor, in patients with non-active secondary progressive multiple sclerosis. The randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial plans to enroll approximately 990 subjects with a 2:1 randomization scheme. Treatment duration will range from 24 to 60 months, with a minimum required treatment period of 12 months.

Why it matters
This Phase 3 trial establishes Zenas BioPharma as a direct competitor in progressive MS, targeting non-active SPMS where high unmet need remains and competing against other clinical-stage BTK inhibitors developed by Sanofi, Roche, and Merck KGaA.
