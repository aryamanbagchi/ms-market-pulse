Editor's Take

The high-impact narrative this week centers on a major push by Zenas BioPharma and Novartis to establish BTK inhibition in progressive multiple sclerosis, a traditionally underserved segment with high unmet need. Zenas initiated two global Phase 3 trials for orelabrutinib in primary progressive and non-active secondary progressive MS, while Novartis opened Phase 3 recruitment for remibrutinib in secondary progressive MS. This collective momentum highlights an industry-wide pivot toward targeting compartmentalized neuroinflammation as legacy franchise revenues decline, evidenced by Biogen reporting that newer non-MS assets have overtaken its core MS business. Meanwhile, Roche is reinforcing its dominant anti-CD20 position by advancing a Phase 3 study evaluating a higher intravenous dose of ocrelizumab to maximize clinical efficacy against relapsing forms. Strategy teams should closely track enrollment velocity across these progressive MS BTKi trials to gauge how rapidly this novel mechanism could disrupt the market dynamic.
