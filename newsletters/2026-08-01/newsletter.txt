EDITOR'S TAKE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A major clinical push into progressive multiple sclerosis is underway as BTK
inhibitors target non-relapsing populations with high unmet need. Zenas
BioPharma initiated dual Phase 3 trials evaluating its CNS-penetrable
candidate orelabrutinib in primary and non-active secondary progressive MS,
while Novartis launched its own Phase 3 study for remibrutinib in SPMS.
Concurrently, the anti-CD20 landscape is shifting as Roche advances a high-
dose ocrelizumab regimen to extend product defense against emerging
competition like Amgen's biosimilar candidate, ABP 692. On the commercial
front, Australian policy decisions exempted MS therapies from mandatory price
cuts, contrasting with Biogen's latest earnings where non-MS revenues eclipsed
its declining legacy MS business. Strategy teams must now watch enrollment
trajectories across these late-stage BTK trials to see if the mechanism can
finally deliver meaningful efficacy in progressive disease.
