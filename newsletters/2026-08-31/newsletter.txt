EDITOR'S TAKE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Zenas BioPharma has aggressively expanded the late-stage progressive MS
pipeline by initiating two global Phase 3 trials for its CNS-penetrable BTK
inhibitor, orelabrutinib, targeting primary progressive and non-active
secondary progressive populations against placebo. This double move
underscores a broader competitive effort across the market to challenge
dominant anti-CD20 therapies and capture non-relapsing indications where unmet
need remains highest. Concurrently, both Novartis and TG Therapeutics are
running Phase 3b trials designed to carve out market share from established
anti-CD20 regimens: Novartis is testing remibrutinib directly in patients
switching off ocrelizumab, while TG Therapeutics is evaluating modified dosing
schedules for ublituximab, including in patients with suboptimal prior anti-
CD20 responses. Together, these developments signal a pivotal shift toward
head-to-head positioning against anti-CD20 standard-of-care and expansion into
progressive forms of the disease. Industry observers should watch for
enrollment momentum across Zenas's progressive MS programs and initial switch
dynamics from Novartis's remibrutinib trial.

1. A Study of Orelabrutinib in Patients With Primary Progressive Multiple
Sclerosis
   Impact 4/5 | ClinicalTrials.gov | 28 Aug 2026 | Phase 3 | Recruiting |
   Zenas BioPharma (USA), LLC | NCT07067463

   Zenas BioPharma has initiated recruitment for a global Phase 3 trial
   (NCT07067463) evaluating its CNS-penetrable BTK inhibitor, orelabrutinib,
   in patients with primary progressive multiple sclerosis (PPMS). The
   randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study will enroll
   approximately 705 subjects in a 2:1 ratio, with treatment durations ranging
   from 30 to 60 months and a minimum duration of 12 months.

   WHY IT MATTERS: The advancement of orelabrutinib into Phase 3 development
   introduces a novel oral BTK inhibitor into the primary progressive MS
   pipeline, directly threatening Roche's market exclusivity with Ocrevus in
   PPMS and intensifying late-stage competition among BTKi developers.
   https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07067463

2. Study to Evaluate Safety, Efficacy and Pharmacokinetics (PK) of a Modified
Regimen of Ublituximab
   Impact 4/5 | ClinicalTrials.gov | 27 Aug 2026 | Phase 3 | Recruiting | TG
   Therapeutics, Inc. | NCT05877963

   TG Therapeutics is currently recruiting participants for a Phase 3b trial
   (NCT05877963) evaluating a modified regimen of its anti-CD20 therapy,
   ublituximab, in relapsing multiple sclerosis (RMS). The three-part study
   assesses efficacy based on T1 Gadolinium-enhancing lesions in Part A,
   pharmacokinetics in Part B, and efficacy in patients with a suboptimal
   experience on prior anti-CD20 therapy in Part C. Part A and Part C are
   single-arm open-label, while Part B is a randomized, double-blind, placebo-
   controlled design.

   WHY IT MATTERS: Demonstrating clinical utility for a modified regimen or in
   patients with prior suboptimal anti-CD20 response could differentiate TG
   Therapeutics' ublituximab against entrenched anti-CD20 competitors like
   Roche's Ocrevus and Novartis' Kesimpta.
   https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05877963

3. Efficacy and Safety of Remibrutinib After Switching From Ocrelizumab in
Participants Living With Relapsing Multiple Sclerosis.
   Impact 4/5 | ClinicalTrials.gov | 26 Aug 2026 | Phase 3 | Recruiting |
   Novartis Pharmaceuticals | NCT06846281

   Novartis has initiated recruitment for a Phase 3b study (NCT06846281)
   evaluating remibrutinib in patients with relapsing multiple sclerosis. The
   trial assesses the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of switching patients
   to remibrutinib following prior ocrelizumab therapy compared to maintaining
   continuous ocrelizumab treatment.

   WHY IT MATTERS: A successful switch strategy could allow Novartis to
   position its oral BTK inhibitor remibrutinib directly against Roche's
   market-leading anti-CD20 therapy ocrelizumab in relapsing MS.
   https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06846281

4. A Study of Orelabrutinib in Patients With Secondary Progressive Multiple
Sclerosis
   Impact 4/5 | ClinicalTrials.gov | 25 Aug 2026 | Phase 3 | Recruiting |
   Zenas BioPharma (USA), LLC | NCT07299019

   Zenas BioPharma has initiated recruitment for a global Phase 3 trial
   (NCT07299019) evaluating its CNS-penetrable BTK inhibitor, orelabrutinib,
   in non-active secondary progressive multiple sclerosis. The randomized,
   double-blind study will enroll approximately 990 patients in a 2:1 ratio
   against placebo, with treatment duration ranging from 24 to 60 months
   (minimum 12 months).

   WHY IT MATTERS: Zenas BioPharma's Phase 3 trial advances a novel CNS-
   penetrable BTK inhibitor into the non-active SPMS space, threatening
   established progressive MS pipelines and expanding competition against
   late-stage BTKi developers like Sanofi and Roche.
   https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07299019
