EDITOR'S TAKE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Novartis suffered a major pipeline setback as weak relapse data for its oral
BTK inhibitor remibrutinib disappointed expectations, disrupting the company's
growth strategy in relapsing MS. This vulnerability accelerates a broader
shift in the BTK inhibitor landscape, where Zenas BioPharma is aggressively
advancing orelabrutinib into primary and secondary progressive MS with two
global Phase 3 initiations, while Roche prepares for an impending FDA decision
on fenebrutinib. Meanwhile, safety liabilities came into focus for high-
potency modalities following Bristol Myers Squibb's report of a patient death
in its CAR T MS trial and emerging data linking anti-CD20 therapies to
inflammatory bowel disease. Meanwhile, TG Therapeutics continues to fortify
its anti-CD20 franchise by progressing subcutaneous ublituximab through Phase
3 trials to challenge established intravenous options. Strategic attention now
centers on Roche's upcoming regulatory decision and early safety reporting
from high-intensity cell therapy trials to determine how market share will
reallocate.
