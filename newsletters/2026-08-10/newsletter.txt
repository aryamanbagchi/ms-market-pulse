EDITOR'S TAKE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Zenas BioPharma has aggressively expanded the late-stage BTK inhibitor
battlefield by initiating recruitment for two global Phase 3 trials evaluating
orelabrutinib in non-active secondary progressive and primary progressive
multiple sclerosis. This dual pivot toward progressive forms of MS highlights
a broader strategic shift across the market away from crowded relapsing-
remitting indications and toward progressive diseases with high unmet need.
Concurrently, Sanofi is safeguarding its own pipeline flexibility by launching
a Phase 3 non-inferiority study for a subcutaneous formulation of its CD40L
inhibitor frexalimab across both relapsing and non-relapsing SPMS cohorts.
Meanwhile, convenience-driven reformulations of established therapies continue
to mature, marked by Mapi Pharma's completion of its Phase 3 study for GA
Depot, a monthly glatiramer acetate injection. Strategic focus now turns to
whether orelabrutinib's CNS penetrance can deliver clinical differentiation
over placebo in progressive populations across multi-year exposure windows.

1. A Study of Orelabrutinib in Patients With Primary Progressive Multiple
Sclerosis
   Impact 4/5 | ClinicalTrials.gov | 07 Aug 2026 | Phase 3 | Recruiting |
   Zenas BioPharma (USA), LLC | NCT07067463

   Zenas BioPharma has initiated recruitment for a global, randomized, double-
   blind Phase 3 study (NCT07067463) evaluating its CNS-penetrable BTK
   inhibitor, orelabrutinib, against placebo in primary progressive multiple
   sclerosis (PPMS). The trial will enroll approximately 705 subjects
   randomized 2:1, with treatment durations ranging from 30 to 60 months
   (minimum 12 months).

   WHY IT MATTERS: Orelabrutinib's progression into Phase 3 for PPMS advances
   a new CNS-penetrant BTK inhibitor into direct competition with established
   and pipeline progressive MS disease-modifying therapies.
   https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07067463

2. A Study of Orelabrutinib in Patients With Secondary Progressive Multiple
Sclerosis
   Impact 4/5 | ClinicalTrials.gov | 06 Aug 2026 | Phase 3 | Recruiting |
   Zenas BioPharma (USA), LLC | NCT07299019

   Zenas BioPharma has initiated recruitment for a global Phase 3 trial
   (NCT07299019) evaluating orelabrutinib, a CNS-penetrable BTK inhibitor, in
   patients with non-active secondary progressive multiple sclerosis. The
   randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study will enroll
   approximately 990 subjects in a 2:1 ratio to receive treatment for 24 to 60
   months, with a minimum duration of 12 months.

   WHY IT MATTERS: Zenas BioPharma is advancing a CNS-penetrable BTK inhibitor
   directly into Phase 3 for non-active SPMS, placing it in competition with
   other BTK inhibitor developers targeting progressive MS populations.
   https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07299019

3. Non-inferiority Study of Frexalimab Subcutaneous Administration Compared to
Intravenous Administration in Adult Participants With Multiple Sclerosis
   Impact 4/5 | ClinicalTrials.gov | 05 Aug 2026 | Phase 3 | Recruiting |
   Sanofi | NCT07325292

   Sanofi has initiated a randomized, open-label Phase 3 trial (NCT07325292)
   evaluating the non-inferiority of subcutaneous frexalimab administered
   every 4 weeks compared to intravenous administration every 4 weeks. The
   48-week study is currently recruiting adults aged 18 to 60 with relapsing
   multiple sclerosis (RMS) and non-relapsing secondary progressive multiple
   sclerosis (nrSPMS). An optional extension (Part C) will continue until a
   long-term safety study begins.

   WHY IT MATTERS: A successful subcutaneous non-inferiority readout would
   enable Sanofi to offer a more convenient route of administration for
   frexalimab, enhancing its competitive positioning against IV- and SC-
   administered disease-modifying therapies in RMS and nrSPMS.
   https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07325292

4. A Study to Asses Efficacy, Safety and Tolerability of Monthly Long-acting
IM Injection of GA Depot in Subjects With RMS
   Impact 4/5 | ClinicalTrials.gov | 05 Aug 2026 | Phase 3 | Completed | Mapi
   Pharma Ltd. | NCT04121221

   Mapi Pharma has completed its multinational, randomized, double-blind,
   placebo-controlled Phase 3 trial (NCT04121221) evaluating GA Depot, a long-
   acting glatiramer acetate intramuscular injection administered once every
   four weeks. The study assessed the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of
   the monthly formulation in patients with Relapsing Forms of Multiple
   Sclerosis (RMS).

   WHY IT MATTERS: A successful monthly glatiramer acetate depot offers a
   reduced dosing frequency compared to daily or thrice-weekly Copaxone
   formulations, potentially disrupting the glatiramer acetate market segment
   and challenging established RMS disease-modifying therapies.
   https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04121221

5. A Study to Evaluate Efficacy, Safety, and Tolerability of Alemtuzumab in
Pediatric Patients With RRMS With Disease Activity on Prior DMT
   Impact 3/5 | ClinicalTrials.gov | 04 Aug 2026 | Phase 3 | Terminated |
   Genzyme, a Sanofi Company | NCT03368664

   Sanofi company Genzyme has terminated its Phase 3 trial (NCT03368664)
   evaluating intravenous alemtuzumab in pediatric patients aged 10 to under
   18 with relapsing-remitting multiple sclerosis (RRMS) who experienced
   disease activity on prior disease-modifying therapy. The study was designed
   to evaluate efficacy, safety, and tolerability as primary objectives, along
   with pharmacokinetics, pharmacodynamics, anti-drug antibody formation,
   cognition, and quality-of-life outcomes as secondary objectives.

   WHY IT MATTERS: The termination halts Sanofi's clinical development of
   alemtuzumab in pediatric RRMS patients who fail initial disease-modifying
   therapies, limiting the drug's label expansion opportunities in younger
   populations.
   https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03368664
