Editor's Take

Zenas BioPharma has aggressively expanded the late-stage BTK inhibitor battlefield by initiating recruitment for two global Phase 3 trials evaluating orelabrutinib in non-active secondary progressive and primary progressive multiple sclerosis. This dual pivot toward progressive forms of MS highlights a broader strategic shift across the market away from crowded relapsing-remitting indications and toward progressive diseases with high unmet need. Concurrently, Sanofi is safeguarding its own pipeline flexibility by launching a Phase 3 non-inferiority study for a subcutaneous formulation of its CD40L inhibitor frexalimab across both relapsing and non-relapsing SPMS cohorts. Meanwhile, convenience-driven reformulations of established therapies continue to mature, marked by Mapi Pharma's completion of its Phase 3 study for GA Depot, a monthly glatiramer acetate injection. Strategic focus now turns to whether orelabrutinib's CNS penetrance can deliver clinical differentiation over placebo in progressive populations across multi-year exposure windows.

A Study of Orelabrutinib in Patients With Primary Progressive Multiple Sclerosis IMPACT 4/5
ClinicalTrials.gov · 07 Aug 2026 · Phase 3 · Recruiting · Zenas BioPharma (USA), LLC · NCT07067463

Zenas BioPharma has initiated recruitment for a global, randomized, double-blind Phase 3 study (NCT07067463) evaluating its CNS-penetrable BTK inhibitor, orelabrutinib, against placebo in primary progressive multiple sclerosis (PPMS). The trial will enroll approximately 705 subjects randomized 2:1, with treatment durations ranging from 30 to 60 months (minimum 12 months).

Why it matters
Orelabrutinib's progression into Phase 3 for PPMS advances a new CNS-penetrant BTK inhibitor into direct competition with established and pipeline progressive MS disease-modifying therapies.

A Study of Orelabrutinib in Patients With Secondary Progressive Multiple Sclerosis IMPACT 4/5
ClinicalTrials.gov · 06 Aug 2026 · Phase 3 · Recruiting · Zenas BioPharma (USA), LLC · NCT07299019

Zenas BioPharma has initiated recruitment for a global Phase 3 trial (NCT07299019) evaluating orelabrutinib, a CNS-penetrable BTK inhibitor, in patients with non-active secondary progressive multiple sclerosis. The randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study will enroll approximately 990 subjects in a 2:1 ratio to receive treatment for 24 to 60 months, with a minimum duration of 12 months.

Why it matters
Zenas BioPharma is advancing a CNS-penetrable BTK inhibitor directly into Phase 3 for non-active SPMS, placing it in competition with other BTK inhibitor developers targeting progressive MS populations.

Non-inferiority Study of Frexalimab Subcutaneous Administration Compared to Intravenous Administration in Adult Participants With Multiple Sclerosis IMPACT 4/5
ClinicalTrials.gov · 05 Aug 2026 · Phase 3 · Recruiting · Sanofi · NCT07325292

Sanofi has initiated a randomized, open-label Phase 3 trial (NCT07325292) evaluating the non-inferiority of subcutaneous frexalimab administered every 4 weeks compared to intravenous administration every 4 weeks. The 48-week study is currently recruiting adults aged 18 to 60 with relapsing multiple sclerosis (RMS) and non-relapsing secondary progressive multiple sclerosis (nrSPMS). An optional extension (Part C) will continue until a long-term safety study begins.

Why it matters
A successful subcutaneous non-inferiority readout would enable Sanofi to offer a more convenient route of administration for frexalimab, enhancing its competitive positioning against IV- and SC-administered disease-modifying therapies in RMS and nrSPMS.

A Study to Asses Efficacy, Safety and Tolerability of Monthly Long-acting IM Injection of GA Depot in Subjects With RMS IMPACT 4/5
ClinicalTrials.gov · 05 Aug 2026 · Phase 3 · Completed · Mapi Pharma Ltd. · NCT04121221

Mapi Pharma has completed its multinational, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Phase 3 trial (NCT04121221) evaluating GA Depot, a long-acting glatiramer acetate intramuscular injection administered once every four weeks. The study assessed the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of the monthly formulation in patients with Relapsing Forms of Multiple Sclerosis (RMS).

Why it matters
A successful monthly glatiramer acetate depot offers a reduced dosing frequency compared to daily or thrice-weekly Copaxone formulations, potentially disrupting the glatiramer acetate market segment and challenging established RMS disease-modifying therapies.

A Study to Evaluate Efficacy, Safety, and Tolerability of Alemtuzumab in Pediatric Patients With RRMS With Disease Activity on Prior DMT IMPACT 3/5
ClinicalTrials.gov · 04 Aug 2026 · Phase 3 · Terminated · Genzyme, a Sanofi Company · NCT03368664

Sanofi company Genzyme has terminated its Phase 3 trial (NCT03368664) evaluating intravenous alemtuzumab in pediatric patients aged 10 to under 18 with relapsing-remitting multiple sclerosis (RRMS) who experienced disease activity on prior disease-modifying therapy. The study was designed to evaluate efficacy, safety, and tolerability as primary objectives, along with pharmacokinetics, pharmacodynamics, anti-drug antibody formation, cognition, and quality-of-life outcomes as secondary objectives.

Why it matters
The termination halts Sanofi's clinical development of alemtuzumab in pediatric RRMS patients who fail initial disease-modifying therapies, limiting the drug's label expansion opportunities in younger populations.
