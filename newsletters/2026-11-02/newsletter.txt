EDITOR'S TAKE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Zenas BioPharma's dual Phase 3 initiations for its CNS-penetrable BTK
inhibitor, orelabrutinib, in primary progressive and non-active secondary
progressive MS mark a decisive shift toward high-unmet-need progressive
populations. This focus on advanced disease is further reflected by Sundance
Biosciences advancing SUN-627 into Phase 2a safety trials for progressive
cohorts. Concurrently, TG Therapeutics is defending and extending its
ublituximab franchise through a Phase 2/3 pediatric relapsing trial alongside
Phase 2 subcutaneous autoinjector studies designed to improve delivery
convenience. Beyond disease modification, Celgene is targeting symptomatic
management by actively recruiting a Phase 2 trial for BMS-986368 in MS
spasticity. Strategy teams should watch whether Zenas can sustain rapid
enrollment across its ambitious progressive MS studies, where clinical
efficacy has historically proven difficult to establish.

1. Study to Assess Effects of Ublituximab in Pediatric Participants With
Relapsing Forms of Multiple Sclerosis
   Impact 4/5 | ClinicalTrials.gov | 29 Oct 2026 | Phase 2, Phase 3 |
   Recruiting | TG Therapeutics, Inc. | NCT07220252

   TG Therapeutics has initiated a Phase 2/3 trial (NCT07220252) evaluating
   ublituximab in pediatric participants aged 10 to under 18 years with
   relapsing forms of multiple sclerosis (RMS). Part A will assess the
   pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of ublituximab, Part B will evaluate
   its non-inferiority against fingolimod, and Part C will examine long-term
   safety and efficacy during an extension period.

   WHY IT MATTERS: If successful, TG Therapeutics could expand ublituximab's
   label into pediatric RMS, directly competing against Novartis's fingolimod
   and established anti-CD20 therapies in a specialized patient segment.
   https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07220252

2. A Study of Orelabrutinib in Patients With Primary Progressive Multiple
Sclerosis
   Impact 4/5 | ClinicalTrials.gov | 27 Oct 2026 | Phase 3 | Recruiting |
   Zenas BioPharma (USA), LLC | NCT07067463

   Zenas BioPharma has initiated recruitment for a global Phase 3 clinical
   trial (NCT07067463) evaluating its CNS-penetrable BTK inhibitor,
   orelabrutinib, in primary progressive multiple sclerosis (PPMS). The
   randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study plans to enroll
   approximately 705 subjects in a 2:1 ratio, with treatment durations ranging
   from 30 to 60 months and a minimum exposure of 12 months.

   WHY IT MATTERS: Progress in Phase 3 trials for orelabrutinib advances
   another CNS-penetrating BTK inhibitor into late-stage development for PPMS,
   intensifying competition for established players like Roche and other BTK
   developers targeting non-relapsing disease populations.
   https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07067463

3. A Study of Orelabrutinib in Patients With Secondary Progressive Multiple
Sclerosis
   Impact 4/5 | ClinicalTrials.gov | 27 Oct 2026 | Phase 3 | Recruiting |
   Zenas BioPharma (USA), LLC | NCT07299019

   Zenas BioPharma has initiated recruitment for a global Phase 3, randomized,
   double-blind study (NCT07299019) evaluating the CNS-penetrable BTK
   inhibitor orelabrutinib in non-active secondary progressive multiple
   sclerosis. The multicenter trial aims to enroll approximately 990 patients
   in a 2:1 ratio against placebo, with treatment duration ranging from 24 to
   60 months and a minimum treatment period of 12 months.

   WHY IT MATTERS: This trial advances a late-stage BTK inhibitor into the
   non-active SPMS population, directly challenging established and pipeline
   therapies targeting progressive disease forms with limited treatment
   options.
   https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07299019

4. A Phase 2a Study of SUN-627 in Participants With Non-Active Secondary
Progressive and Primary Progressive Multiple Sclerosis (SPMS/PPMS)
   Impact 3/5 | ClinicalTrials.gov | 30 Oct 2026 | Phase 2 | Recruiting |
   Sundance Biosciences, Inc. | NCT07840326

   Sundance Biosciences has initiated recruitment for a Phase 2a clinical
   trial evaluating SUN-627 in adult patients aged 18 to 70 with non-active
   secondary progressive (SPMS) and primary progressive multiple sclerosis
   (PPMS). The study's primary objectives focus on assessing the safety and
   tolerability of the asset in these progressive MS populations.

   WHY IT MATTERS: Sundance Biosciences' Phase 2a trial targeting non-active
   SPMS and PPMS addresses a high-unmet-need segment where established anti-
   CD20 and DMT options offer limited efficacy.
   https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07840326

5. A Study to Evaluate Pharmacokinetics (PK) and Safety of Subcutaneous (SC)
Ublituximab Administered at Various Injection Sites and Relative
Bioavailability Via Autoinjector (AI) Versus Syringe Subcutaneously in
Participants With Multiple Sclerosis (MS)
   Impact 3/5 | ClinicalTrials.gov | 29 Oct 2026 | Phase 2 | Recruiting | TG
   Therapeutics, Inc. | NCT07503873

   TG Therapeutics has initiated recruitment for a Phase 2 trial (NCT07503873)
   evaluating a subcutaneous formulation of ublituximab in participants with
   Multiple Sclerosis. The study will assess the asset's pharmacokinetics and
   safety across various administration sites, as well as its relative
   bioavailability when delivered via a prefilled autoinjector pen versus a
   syringe. The primary focus of the trial is establishing the pharmacokinetic
   profile and delivery dynamics for SC administration.

   WHY IT MATTERS: Successful subcutaneous autoinjector delivery of
   ublituximab would enhance patient convenience and positioning for TG
   Therapeutics against established anti-CD20 therapies like Roche's Ocrevus
   and Novartis's Kesimpta.
   https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07503873

6. A Study to Evaluate the Efficacy, Safety and Tolerability of BMS-986368 in
Participants With Multiple Sclerosis Spasticity
   Impact 3/5 | ClinicalTrials.gov | 27 Oct 2026 | Phase 2 | Recruiting |
   Celgene | NCT06782490

   Celgene is actively recruiting participants for a Phase 2 clinical trial
   (NCT06782490) to evaluate BMS-986368. The study aims to assess the
   efficacy, safety, and tolerability of the asset specifically in patients
   presenting with multiple sclerosis spasticity. Primary completion and study
   completion are anticipated for late 2026.

   WHY IT MATTERS: This trial positions Bristol Myers Squibb/Celgene to
   potentially expand its MS portfolio into symptomatic management by
   targeting spasticity, a high-unmet-need complication of the disease.
   https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06782490
