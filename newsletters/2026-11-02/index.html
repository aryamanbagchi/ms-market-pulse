Editor's Take

Zenas BioPharma's dual Phase 3 initiations for its CNS-penetrable BTK inhibitor, orelabrutinib, in primary progressive and non-active secondary progressive MS mark a decisive shift toward high-unmet-need progressive populations. This focus on advanced disease is further reflected by Sundance Biosciences advancing SUN-627 into Phase 2a safety trials for progressive cohorts. Concurrently, TG Therapeutics is defending and extending its ublituximab franchise through a Phase 2/3 pediatric relapsing trial alongside Phase 2 subcutaneous autoinjector studies designed to improve delivery convenience. Beyond disease modification, Celgene is targeting symptomatic management by actively recruiting a Phase 2 trial for BMS-986368 in MS spasticity. Strategy teams should watch whether Zenas can sustain rapid enrollment across its ambitious progressive MS studies, where clinical efficacy has historically proven difficult to establish.

Study to Assess Effects of Ublituximab in Pediatric Participants With Relapsing Forms of Multiple Sclerosis IMPACT 4/5
ClinicalTrials.gov · 29 Oct 2026 · Phase 2, Phase 3 · Recruiting · TG Therapeutics, Inc. · NCT07220252

TG Therapeutics has initiated a Phase 2/3 trial (NCT07220252) evaluating ublituximab in pediatric participants aged 10 to under 18 years with relapsing forms of multiple sclerosis (RMS). Part A will assess the pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics of ublituximab, Part B will evaluate its non-inferiority against fingolimod, and Part C will examine long-term safety and efficacy during an extension period.

Why it matters
If successful, TG Therapeutics could expand ublituximab's label into pediatric RMS, directly competing against Novartis's fingolimod and established anti-CD20 therapies in a specialized patient segment.

A Study of Orelabrutinib in Patients With Primary Progressive Multiple Sclerosis IMPACT 4/5
ClinicalTrials.gov · 27 Oct 2026 · Phase 3 · Recruiting · Zenas BioPharma (USA), LLC · NCT07067463

Zenas BioPharma has initiated recruitment for a global Phase 3 clinical trial (NCT07067463) evaluating its CNS-penetrable BTK inhibitor, orelabrutinib, in primary progressive multiple sclerosis (PPMS). The randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study plans to enroll approximately 705 subjects in a 2:1 ratio, with treatment durations ranging from 30 to 60 months and a minimum exposure of 12 months.

Why it matters
Progress in Phase 3 trials for orelabrutinib advances another CNS-penetrating BTK inhibitor into late-stage development for PPMS, intensifying competition for established players like Roche and other BTK developers targeting non-relapsing disease populations.

A Study of Orelabrutinib in Patients With Secondary Progressive Multiple Sclerosis IMPACT 4/5
ClinicalTrials.gov · 27 Oct 2026 · Phase 3 · Recruiting · Zenas BioPharma (USA), LLC · NCT07299019

Zenas BioPharma has initiated recruitment for a global Phase 3, randomized, double-blind study (NCT07299019) evaluating the CNS-penetrable BTK inhibitor orelabrutinib in non-active secondary progressive multiple sclerosis. The multicenter trial aims to enroll approximately 990 patients in a 2:1 ratio against placebo, with treatment duration ranging from 24 to 60 months and a minimum treatment period of 12 months.

Why it matters
This trial advances a late-stage BTK inhibitor into the non-active SPMS population, directly challenging established and pipeline therapies targeting progressive disease forms with limited treatment options.

A Study to Evaluate Pharmacokinetics (PK) and Safety of Subcutaneous (SC) Ublituximab Administered at Various Injection Sites and Relative Bioavailability Via Autoinjector (AI) Versus Syringe Subcutaneously in Participants With Multiple Sclerosis (MS) IMPACT 3/5
ClinicalTrials.gov · 29 Oct 2026 · Phase 2 · Recruiting · TG Therapeutics, Inc. · NCT07503873

TG Therapeutics has initiated recruitment for a Phase 2 trial (NCT07503873) evaluating a subcutaneous formulation of ublituximab in participants with Multiple Sclerosis. The study will assess the asset's pharmacokinetics and safety across various administration sites, as well as its relative bioavailability when delivered via a prefilled autoinjector pen versus a syringe. The primary focus of the trial is establishing the pharmacokinetic profile and delivery dynamics for SC administration.

Why it matters
Successful subcutaneous autoinjector delivery of ublituximab would enhance patient convenience and positioning for TG Therapeutics against established anti-CD20 therapies like Roche's Ocrevus and Novartis's Kesimpta.

ClinicalTrials.gov · 27 Oct 2026 · Phase 2 · Recruiting · Celgene · NCT06782490

Celgene is actively recruiting participants for a Phase 2 clinical trial (NCT06782490) to evaluate BMS-986368. The study aims to assess the efficacy, safety, and tolerability of the asset specifically in patients presenting with multiple sclerosis spasticity.
