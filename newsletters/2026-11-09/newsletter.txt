EDITOR'S TAKE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Zenas BioPharma has significantly escalated the competitive pressure in
progressive multiple sclerosis by initiating twin global Phase 3 trials for
its CNS-penetrable BTK inhibitor, orelabrutinib. These pivotal studies
targeting non-active secondary progressive MS (NCT07299019) and primary
progressive MS (NCT07067463) highlight an industry-wide pivot toward
addressing non-relapsing forms of the disease where unmet need remains
highest. Meanwhile, Biogen faces commercial headwinds as regulatory watchdogs
investigate allegations that the drugmaker restricted access to a critical MS
diagnostic test. Together, these events underscore how the market is shifting
simultaneously on two fronts: clinical innovation moving deeper into
progressive disease, and heightened scrutiny over commercial access
strategies. Moving forward, strategy teams must track whether orelabrutinib's
CNS penetration translates into superior long-term disability outcomes in its
multi-year placebo-controlled trials.
